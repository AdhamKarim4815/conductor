# /email

Run the Conductor workflow with this preset.

1. Read her profile (writing voice) and playbook. If the email involves a course, read that course pack for the professor's name and policies.
2. Ask what you need, in one round:
   - Who it is to and her relationship with them.
   - What she wants to happen (the ask) and any deadline.
   - Key facts to include; if replying, have her paste or point to the original message.
   - Tone: formal, friendly, firm.
3. Dispatch the writer with a full brief, including a sample of her voice from the profile.
4. For professors, recruiters or anything with consequences, run the strict-checker.
5. Deliver subject and body ready to copy. If an email app is connected, offer to save it as a draft; never send without her explicit yes.
