# /apply

Run the Conductor workflow with this preset.

1. Read her profile and playbook. If she has a resume in her Conductor folder or attached, use it as the source of truth about her experience.
2. Ask what you need, in one round:
   - The firm, role, and posting (link or pasted text).
   - What she needs: cover letter, resume tailoring, networking message, application answers, or interview prep.
   - Deadline, and anyone she has already spoken to at the firm.
3. Dispatch in parallel: a researcher on the firm, group and recent deals or news; and, once that is back, the writer with the research and her real experience. Never invent experience, grades or dates.
4. For interview prep, have the tutor build technical questions (accounting, valuation, markets) with model answers, and the researcher build firm-specific questions.
5. Run the strict-checker on anything she will submit.
6. Deliver as a doc she can edit. Offer to track the application in a sheet if she is applying to several firms.
