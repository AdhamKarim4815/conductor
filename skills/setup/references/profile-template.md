# Profile template

Save as `profile.md` in her Conductor folder.

```markdown
# Profile
Last updated: <YYYY-MM-DD>

## About
Program, year, specialist/major/minor, goals (internships, recruiting, clubs).

## Courses this term
Codes with one line each; details live in courses/.

## How she likes answers
Length, tone, detail, formatting, spelling, things to always or never do.

## How she learns best
Explanations vs examples, practice, flashcards, visuals.

## Writing voice
How she writes emails and messages, typical phrases, one or two short samples.

## What she uses Claude for
Typical requests in her own words.

## Recurring tasks and deadlines

## Tools
Connected apps, installed skills, devices she uses.
```
