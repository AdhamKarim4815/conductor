# Course pack template

Save as `courses/<CODE>.md` in her Conductor folder. Keep it factual and current; date every update.

```markdown
# <CODE>: <Course name>
Term: <Fall 2026>  |  Section: <L0101>  |  Last updated: <YYYY-MM-DD>
Quercus: <course link>

## People
- Professor: <name>, <email>, office hours <when, where>
- TAs: <names and emails if listed>

## Grading
| Component | Weight | Date | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|

## Upcoming (next 4 weeks)
- <date>: <assessment>, <weight>, <format, what it covers>

## Policies
- AI use: <exact short quote or precise summary>
- Collaboration: <rule>
- Late work / missed work: <rule>

## Assignment briefs
### <Assignment name>
- Due: <date>, weight <x%>
- Task: <summary of instructions>
- Rubric: <criteria and points>
- Source file: courses/<CODE>/<file>

## Topics by week
| Week | Topic | Readings |
|---|---|---|

## Key concepts and formulas
Written in KaTeX, in the course's own notation.

## How this course is taught
Professor's emphasis, question styles on past tests, notation quirks, anything she found hard.
```
