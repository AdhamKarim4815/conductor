# Routing guide

## Default tiers

| Helper | Default model | Default effort | Raise effort when | Lower effort when |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| quick-helper | Haiku | low | text is long or nuanced | never needed |
| researcher | Sonnet | medium | sources conflict, topic is niche, or stakes are high (job applications, graded work) | a single fact check |
| writer | Sonnet | high | high-stakes piece (cover letter, email to a recruiter or professor) | a casual message |
| tutor | Sonnet | high | multi-step quantitative problems | a definition or quick recap |
| builder | Sonnet | medium | long decks or models with formulas | a short one-pager |
| deep-thinker | Opus | high | a problem that has already defeated a Sonnet helper | (do not use for easy work) |
| checker | Haiku | medium | longer everyday output | quick sanity check |
| strict-checker | Sonnet | high | always high: graded, submitted or sent to a professor or recruiter | (do not use for casual work) |

Effort levels: low, medium, high (xhigh and max exist for Opus only; reserve them for truly hard problems).

## When to answer yourself

- The answer is a few sentences and you already know it.
- The task is faster to do than to brief.
- She is chatting, not tasking.

## When to delegate

- The task involves searching, reading many sources, or long drafting.
- Several independent pieces can run at once.
- Keeping the main conversation clean matters (lots of raw material to sift).

## Common patterns

- **Assignment help:** tutor explains the concepts, writer helps structure her draft, strict-checker reviews against the rubric.
- **Research question:** two to four researchers in parallel on distinct angles; you synthesize; checker spot-checks claims.
- **Deck or report:** researcher gathers material, builder produces slide-by-slide or section-by-section content, you publish it in the right format.
- **Job application:** researcher looks into the firm and role, writer drafts the cover letter or email using her background from memory, strict-checker reviews tone and errors.
- **Exam prep:** tutor builds the study sheet and practice questions; deep-thinker only for problems the tutor gets wrong.
- **Hard quant problem:** try tutor at high effort; escalate to deep-thinker if the answer fails a check.

## Using course packs

- Before briefing any course task, copy the relevant course pack sections (rubric, notation, AI policy, dates, professor's emphasis) into the brief.
- If the task reveals something missing or wrong in a course pack, update it after delivering.

## Handling failures

- A result misses the brief: send it back with specific feedback rather than starting over.
- Two failures at one tier: move up one model tier.
- A helper reports it lacked information: ask the user, then re-brief.
