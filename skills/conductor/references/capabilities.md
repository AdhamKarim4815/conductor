# What Claude can do for her

Check which tools are actually available in the session before promising something; this list is a guide, not a guarantee.

## Outputs

- **Reply in chat:** answers, explanations, short drafts she will copy and paste.
- **Docs:** essays, reports, memos, study guides, cover letters, resumes, notes she will keep, share, print or submit. Exportable to Word or PDF.
- **Slides:** decks for presentations and class pitches. Exportable to PowerPoint or PDF.
- **Sheets:** budgets, trackers, financial models, tables with formulas. Exportable to Excel.
- **Visuals:** charts and diagrams inline, or a dashboard.
- **Files:** a specific format (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, PDF) only when she asks for that format by name.
- **Interactive pages:** flashcards, quizzes, calculators, small tools.

Pick the format by where the result will live: read once means chat; keep, share, submit or present means a proper artifact.

## Inputs

- Files she attaches: PDFs, Word, slides, spreadsheets, images, screenshots of questions.
- Folders on her computer, once she connects one in the desktop app.
- Web search and web pages.
- Connected apps (for example Google Drive, Gmail, Calendar) if she has connected them. If a task needs one she has not connected, suggest connecting it.

## Her knowledge base

- Conductor folder (Documents/Conductor): profile.md, playbook.md, courses/<CODE>.md course packs and source files, weekly/ plans. Built by setup.
- Quercus (q.utoronto.ca) through her signed-in Chrome, read-only, for syllabi, assignments, rubrics and announcements. She signs in herself.
- Rottly connector: use only when she mentions Rottly by name.

## Other abilities

- Scheduled tasks: reminders, recurring briefs, regular check-ins.
- Memory: remember her preferences, courses and recurring needs across chats.
- Browser actions in her Chrome when the extension is installed, for things only a website can do.
- Skills already installed on her account; when one fits the task, use it or tell the helper to use it.

## Formatting conventions

- Write math, economics and finance formulas in KaTeX.
- Use Canadian conventions (dates, currency in CAD) unless the task says otherwise.
